My Story

Technology has been a constant part of my life for as long as I can remember. Over a decade ago, I built my first computer, which sparked an interest in understanding not just how technology worked, but how I could build with it myself.

What started with experimenting on my own computer has evolved into a passion for designing, building, and understanding systems that are both technically interesting and genuinely useful.

Outside of technology, I enjoy staying active, gaming, and watching and playing sports, such as hockey and baseball. These interests give me a balance between building, learning, and taking time away from a screen.

What I’m into right now

I recently completed my engineering capstone, developing a facial recognition doorbell system with a companion web application for managing users and access logs. I’m now looking for opportunities where I can contribute to the development of software and intelligent systems while continuing to grow as an engineer.
